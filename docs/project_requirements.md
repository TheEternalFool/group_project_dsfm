## Template and reporting

Follow the supplied [course project template](https://github.com/Elwin-Freudiger/project-template). Its structure supports the introduction, data, EDA, methods where relevant, findings/discussion, conclusion and references. Distinguish completed work from planned methods, and interpret results rather than merely displaying code or charts.

## Dataset recommendations, not submission-format requirements

The course recommends:

- At least **1,000 observations** and **8–12 variables**.
- **3–5 numerical**, **2–3 categorical**, **at least one date/time variable where applicable**, and **at least one text variable**.

These are dataset-selection recommendations, not evidence that the SSA data must be padded with arbitrary columns. Document the original variables, justify useful derived measures and explain any departure from the recommendations in relation to the research questions.

A row count alone does **not** guarantee statistical significance or validity. Appropriate observation units, assumptions, sampling/coverage limitations and evaluation design still matter. Do not repeat the source text's stronger statistical claim as an established guarantee.
